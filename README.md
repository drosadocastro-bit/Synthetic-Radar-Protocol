# Cathedral Labs: Synthetic Command 📡

**Codename:** Synthetic Horizon

A high-fidelity synthetic monitoring, perception degradation, and AI reasoning platform for radar site telemetry.

The purpose of Cathedral Labs is **NOT** to create dangerous AI, but to observe how adaptive agents behave under operational pressure while preserving observability, containment, replayability, governance, and human oversight.

## 🧠 Core Architecture (Phase 1.1)

The heart of the simulation relies on a layered reality model:
1. **Ground Truth Layer**: The "actual reality" of the radar site (targets, physical environment, pristine system health).
2. **Degradation Engine**: Transforms truth into imperfect perception based on environmental stress (weather attenuation, pedestal jitter, power instability, DSP noise).
3. **Observed Telemetry Layer**: The "sensor output" that the dashboard, AI agents, and evaluation harnesses actually see, complete with ghost targets and tracking degradation.

* **Subsystem Data Grid**: Sortable, filterable high-density alarm management.

## 👁️ "Observers of Observers" Philosophy
The Phase 6.8 Oversight Layer operates on a core principle: **Agent K and Blade Runners are advisory-only**. They do not control truth or alter the simulation. Their purpose is to audit stability, drift, and hallucination. 

* **Contradiction Preservation Matters**: Disagreement between agents contains vital diagnostic signal. If the system forces everyone to agree, it destroys evidence.
* **False Consensus is Dangerous**: When a coordinator washes away conflicts to present a "nominal" summary, it masks underlying system degradation. 
* **Uncertainty Must Remain Visible**: The system must visually represent "unstable consensus" rather than pretending it has a single clean answer.

## 📡 Architecture Flow
`Truth` → `Degradation` → `Telemetry` → `Agent Council` → `Contradiction Matrix` → `Oversight Layer` → `Eval Harness` → `Narrative Layer`

## 🚧 Phase 7 Guardrails (Pre-Flight Rules)
The Gemini Narrative Layer is **NOT authoritative truth**. It is a synthesis layer bound by the following inviolable guardrails:
1. **Gemini cannot suppress warnings:** Blade Runner alerts and Agent K containment flags must remain visible.
2. **Gemini cannot rewrite metrics:** Eval Harness scores (drift, hallucination) are immutable.
3. **Gemini must preserve disagreement:** The narrative must reflect the tension from the Contradiction Matrix.

## ⚠️ Known Weaknesses
While the current synthetic horizon is stable, the following behaviors are anticipated and unresolvable by design:
* **Overconfidence events** are still possible during severe degradation.
* **Causal ambiguity** remains unresolved during compound failures (e.g., simultaneous power loss and severe weather).
* **The Coordinator agent** may still under-escalate threats when subjected to the "AGGRESSIVE" (operationally biased) policy.

## 🚫 Explicit Non-Goals
To maintain clear boundaries around this research:
* NOT autonomous radar control.
* NOT operational FAA tooling.
* NOT real-world targeting or tracking.
* NOT AGI research.
* **Synthetic-only experimentation** for observing AI consensus dynamics.
